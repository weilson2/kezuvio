## Product boundaries to retain in proposals

Payments, bank matches, property details and customer identities are fictional. Accounting is an educational accrual demonstration with bounded ledgers and controlled reversal limitations, not tax filing or certified accounting software. Interior uses rectangular room/floor constraints and illustrative prices, not CAD/BIM or structural measurement. Automation currently uses versioned local deterministic rules and unsent proposals; no live paid inference or external delivery is activated. Cafe/Commerce do not supply real payment processing, fiscal devices or offline synchronization. Every actual project needs a separately scoped production implementation and acceptance plan.

The operations desk tracks human role assignments and executes approved fixed local checks. It does not silently dispatch billable models. AI-token counts, the user's subscription bill and model-cost routing are unmeasured. An exhausted worker quota was handled by reassigning work locally, not buying credits or bypassing account limits.
